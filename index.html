Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

This policy describes CRASH LAB for Android, package com.brightpatchstudio.crashlab, including versions that use Google Mobile Ads. The currently available ad-free version does not include the advertising practices described below. Ads-enabled versions may show optional rewarded video ads and interstitial ads after a puzzle is completed. The game is intended for the 13+ audience stated on its Google Play listing.

1. Publisher and contact

CRASH LAB is published by Brightpatch Studio. For privacy questions or requests, contact brightpatchstudio@gmail.com.

2. Information kept on your device

The game stores puzzle progress, settings, and your selected age range on your device. You choose one of three broad ranges: UNDER 16, 16–17, or 18+. The game does not ask for your date of birth, does not require an account, and does not send the selected range itself to our servers. The choice is not identity or age verification.

When Google Mobile Ads is enabled, the game uses the selected range to choose an advertising treatment and sends the resulting treatment signals to Google. UNDER 16 is sent with Google’s under-age and child-directed treatment settings and a G content rating. Ages 16–17 use Teen treatment and a PG rating. Ages 18 and over receive standard ad treatment with a PG rating, subject to applicable privacy choices. Regional consent handling is used where applicable. Ads may be available for every age range; the game does not offer an ad-free age option. The selected range is not identity or age verification.

Progress and settings are stored locally; Brightpatch Studio does not operate a game account or cloud-save service. Android may back up eligible app data according to your device and Google account settings. Such backups are managed by Android and Google, not by a Brightpatch Studio game server.

3. Advertising and information processed by Google

Ads-enabled versions use Google Mobile Ads to request optional rewarded video ads and interstitial ads after puzzle completion. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share information such as your device’s IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location; ad and app interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video views; diagnostics, such as launch time and performance information; and device identifiers, such as Advertising ID and App Set ID. The game does not request precise GPS location. Google describes its SDK data uses as including ad delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention, and states that SDK data is encrypted in transit.

The game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check consent requirements and present privacy messages where configured. The UNDER 16 choice sets Google’s under-age-of-consent signal; Google documents that this signal prevents UMP from requesting consent for that user. For the other age ranges, UMP uses its applicable regional consent flow. The available choices depend on the region and message configuration. Where the app offers an Ad Privacy Options entry, you can use it to reopen the relevant privacy choices.

Google and any advertising partners enabled for the app process information under their own terms, privacy disclosures, and applicable choices. Read Google’s Privacy Policy and information about Google advertising. You can review Google ad controls in My Ad Center; those controls may apply to Google services and do not replace consent or privacy choices presented in the game.

Google explains how it retains and deletes data in its data retention policy. Google’s retention and deletion practices apply to information Google holds. Brightpatch Studio does not control Google’s retention periods or deletion of Google-held data.

4. Internet connection and gameplay

Ads-enabled Android versions require a validated internet connection to start a new puzzle, retry, or move to another level. If the connection drops during a puzzle, you can finish the current attempt and its result can be saved locally. Internet access is also used for consent information and ad requests. Interstitial ads may appear after puzzle completion. A solution demonstration can be requested with a rewarded ad, and is unlocked only after Google reports that the reward was earned unless it is already unlocked. If an ad is unavailable or fails to load, ordinary puzzle progress is not blocked.

5. Email and support

If you email us, we receive the information you include, such as your email address, message, and attachments. We use it to respond, resolve the request, and meet applicable obligations. Email is handled by email providers outside the game. We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to handle and resolve the request and meet applicable obligations, then delete it or retain it as required. Send privacy questions or requests to brightpatchstudio@gmail.com.

6. Your choices and local data

  • You can change the selected age range in the game’s settings. Ads remain part of ads-enabled versions for every range.
  • Where a regional privacy message applies, use its available consent and privacy choices. You can reopen Ad Privacy Options in the game when that entry is available.
  • You can clear the app’s storage or uninstall the game to remove locally stored progress and settings. This may permanently remove your progress.
  • Manage Android backup copies through your device or Google account settings.
  • For privacy requests concerning an email you sent to Brightpatch Studio, contact us at the address above. Google-held data is governed by Google’s own privacy and retention processes linked above.

7. Children and teens

The Google Play listing identifies the game’s intended audience as 13+. The game’s age-range choice is used to apply ad treatment; it does not verify identity or date of birth. For UNDER 16, the app sends Google under-age and child-directed treatment signals. The 16–17 choice receives Teen ad treatment. The 18+ choice receives standard ad treatment, subject to applicable privacy choices. Regional consent flows and other settings may apply.

8. This policy website

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process information about requests to the website under its own terms and privacy disclosures. Opening the policy or contacting us by email uses services outside the game.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the game’s information practices change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version of this policy.
