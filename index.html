Privacy Policy

A small escape. Clear privacy information.

Last updated: 30 October 2026 · Applies to the first Android closed-testing release, version 1.0.0, package com.brightpatchstudio.crashlab.

The short version

CRASH LAB is an offline, single-player puzzle game. You do not need an account. This release contains no advertising, in-app purchases, active analytics or developer-operated cloud service. Your progress is saved on your device.

1. Who provides the game

CRASH LAB is published by Brightpatch Studio. For questions about privacy or this policy, email brightpatchstudio@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The game stores information needed to continue playing, including completed levels, stars and best results, your place in each chapter, and sound and motion preferences. These are local game files and preferences. They are not uploaded to a Brightpatch Studio server.

The game does not ask for your name, email address, date of birth, location, contacts, photographs or microphone access. It does not require a user account or social sign-in.

3. Network access, SDKs and sharing

This Android release bundles its levels, artwork, sounds and solution demonstrations. Its release manifest does not request Internet, advertising-ID or network-state permissions. No game-initiated upload of gameplay events, advertising identifiers, analytics or crash reports is enabled.

The game is built with Unity. Unity online analytics and cloud diagnostics are disabled for this release; there is no advertising or purchasing SDK integration. The game does not sell or share player information with advertisers.

Google Play and Android may separately process installation, store, device or diagnostic information under their own settings and policies, and may make stability reports available to developers. That platform processing is separate from an in-game analytics or cloud-save service operated by Brightpatch Studio.

4. Android backup and device transfer

Android may back up or transfer eligible game progress and preferences if backup is enabled on your device. This is managed by Android and your Google account settings, not by a Brightpatch Studio cloud service. Backup availability and restoration depend on your device and settings and are not guaranteed.

Backups can remain after an app is uninstalled. Use your Android and Google backup settings to manage those copies.

5. Support messages

If you choose to contact us by email, we receive the information you send, such as your email address, message and any attachments. We use that correspondence to answer your request and investigate reported issues. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Email is handled outside the game by email providers, including Gmail. We keep correspondence only as needed for support and legitimate record keeping. You may contact the support address to request deletion of your correspondence, subject to any required retention.

6. Your choices and deletion

  • You can play without registering or providing personal details.
  • You can control music, sound and motion preferences in the game.
  • Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes its local progress. This can permanently remove your progress.
  • Manage Android backup copies separately in your device or Google account settings.
  • Contact us if you need help with privacy or support correspondence.

7. Parents and guardians

This release does not request personal information from players or include targeted advertising, accounts or player-to-player messaging. Parents and guardians can contact us with any questions or concerns. The same local-storage and Android-backup information above applies to children using the game.

8. This privacy webpage

This page has no advertising, analytics scripts, third-party fonts or tracking cookies added by Brightpatch Studio. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process website-request information under its own privacy statement. Opening this policy or your email application uses services outside the game.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the game's handling of information changes. If advertising, analytics, online accounts or other online features are introduced, their data practices will be reviewed and disclosed before release. The date and version scope at the top identify this policy's current coverage.
